HOW TO MAKE A POSTER
1. Go to Bounties & Quests and choose a submenu.
2. Click Add to Announcement beside each posting you want.
3. Open Announcement Creator from the top menu.
4. Edit the title, introduction, posting details or closing message if desired. Posters update automatically.
5. Click Copy image and paste into the Discord announcement channel. Or choose Download PNG and attach the saved PNG to Discord.

Each posting has its own illustrated, gold-framed poster. Long postings continue onto additional numbered images. Images export at 900 pixels wide, using the matching subject artwork and original posting details. If image copying is unsupported or blocked by the browser, Download PNG remains available. Copy text instead is available as an alternative.

Drafts remain only in this browser and survive refresh unless browser storage is cleared. Nothing is automatically sent to Discord. Staff still reviews and posts the image manually.
